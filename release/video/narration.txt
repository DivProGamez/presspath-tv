[s01-splash]
This is PressPath TV, a family app for Android TV that measures navigation inside its own interface. Everything you see is the real release APK on an Android TV emulator, controlled only with D-pad presses.

[s02-home]
Home offers the Navigation Lab, Memory Pairs, Flashcards, Progress and Settings. The evidence card is empty, because nothing has been measured yet.

[s03-baseline-1]
The Navigation Lab runs five guided tasks and records real presses, backtracks, edge misses and seconds. Task two: open Science flashcards and reveal an answer. The live bar counts every press. Seven presses, saved as baseline run one.

[s04-baseline-2]
A second baseline run of the same task. Seven presses.

[s05-r1-preview]
Rule R1 fires when a task needs five or more presses in at least two baseline runs. The preview cites both runs before anything changes. The tiles keep their order. Only a Home shortcut is added. Apply.

[s06-adaptive]
Now the adaptive run: up to the new shortcut, OK, and reveal. Three presses.

[s07-before-after]
Next, open Before and After. It compares the medians of the recorded runs: seven presses down to three. That is minus four, or minus 57 percent, measured on this device.

[s08-undo]
Every change is reversible. Undo removes the shortcut, and the recorded runs are kept.

[s09-memory]
Memory Pairs is fully playable with the remote: twelve cards and six pairs, with pairs found and moves. There is no timer and no score.

[s10-flashcards]
Flashcards cover Letters, Numbers and Everyday Science. OK reveals the answer, and Next moves to the next card.

[s11-accessibility]
Back returns focus to where you were. Settings provides High Contrast, with a yellow focus ring on pure black. Large Text enlarges the interface text, and Reduce Motion turns off zoom and transitions. Clear All Local Data always asks for confirmation first, so nothing is deleted by accident.

[s12-closing]
PressPath TV works offline, requests no permissions, and keeps its data on the device. It was verified on an Android TV emulator. It has not been tested on Fire TV hardware. Every press counts. Every path gets smarter.
